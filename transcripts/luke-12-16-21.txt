I want you to be giving to the local church. I want you to be generous. But a Christian should never give if they feel coerced to. You should be giving joyfully with the heart of worship to God. Your giving should be an act of grateful appreciation for the pastors of this church. It should be an investment into the gospel of Christ and it should be cloaked in humility. We don't give for the praise of man. We give for the praise of God. What all this means, practically, is that a pure-hearted, properly motivated giving is more important than how much you give. It's more important than how you give or when you give.

The rich fool stored up treasures for himself. The wise believer stores up treasures for God. One dies clinging to his barns. In modern terms, maybe clinging to their bank accounts. and the other dies rich toward God. Brothers and sisters, one day each and every one of us will stand before the Lord. And on that day, it will not matter how large our barns were. It won't matter how full our accounts were. It will only matter whether you were rich toward God. Everything you have now belongs to him. He gave it and he sustains it. And one day he will ask you how you used it. And so I want to just ask you plainly, are you living like an owner? Do you own all of your stuff? Or are you living like a steward? One who has been graciously given all things and knows that all things belong to God.

If you've not been faithful in giving, I'm sorry, if you have been faithful in giving, be encouraged. Keep going. Your labor is not in vain. Your father sees what you are giving. If you've not been giving, this is not a moment for shame. It's a moment for obedience. It's a moment to trust God. It's a moment to take a step of faith. Don't wait for someday. Don't wait for when things settle down. Start now. Lay up your treasure in heaven and invest in the kingdom and be rich towards God. And then trust your father that will take care of it.
